# AI-generated studio imagery

Created 9 November 2026 with ChatGPT image generation in an authorized logged-in session. Two original images: (1) a photoreal glossy chartreuse pear-shaped jelly on an ivory cyclorama, (2) the matching empty warm off-white studio sweep. No people, logos, text, brands or real product claims. No Gemini/Veo or generated video.

The original JPEG downloads were visually inspected and SHA-256 checked as distinct:
- Jelly: `9bae222157aea85f5f3d4d57b9413e7abeffa7daab7cac6c641129a8da3d5849`
- Empty sweep: `af80f5ec91fe63c8526c50868470c2ce7dfafd608194353c7dcb52a10171754a`

Shipping assets are responsive 1536/960px WebP at quality 78. `studio-reflections.webp` is a 1024×512 equirectangular-style LDR reflection plate: the generated empty sweep is blurred, mirrored for a continuous longitude seam, darkened, and composited with broad softbox cards matching the reference highlights. Three.js PMREM filters it at load time. It is not a measured HDR panorama. The generated hero is the reduced-motion/no-WebGL fallback; real-time fruit is still editable geometry, not an image animation.

Source design credit remains Marina Budarina's [Jelly Study](https://budarina.design/prompts). Its warm off-white, charcoal and oversized Outfit typography intentionally remain unchanged. Physics is an expressive spring model, not a scientific soft-body solver.
